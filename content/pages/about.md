This wiki is where I keep my projects: what they are, why I made them, and what I learned. Some are finished, some are paused, and some are still messy. That's on purpose. The write-ups are as much for future me as for anyone else.

I'm most interested in small, useful tools: things that answer one question well, like [[trail-weather]], or that come from logging something for long enough to learn from it, like [[commute-data]].

If you're working on something similar, I'd like to hear about it.
